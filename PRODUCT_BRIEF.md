# MarketBell

You don't have to babysit the market.

MarketBell watches your portfolio and the market, investigates what matters, and only asks when a decision genuinely needs you. Its measurable benefit is less repeated monitoring and fewer repeated approvals, rather than stock picking performance.

Watch → notice → investigate → independent challenge → deterministic policy → act within authority or ask → explicitly approve learned rule → handle the next matching case → record.

Authority: hard policy > approved standing rules > soft context > model recommendation. No live trading, brokerage endpoints or orders. Phase 1 actions prepare illustrative consideration notes only.

The core demonstration is PSX → Correct & teach → review fixed example rule → explicit save → VLO handled under that rule. Approval and rejection work independently. The public interface is calm, warm, mobile usable, and centered on Needs You and Handled for You.

Phase 1 uses synthetic scenarios and browser-local history. It must never imply a verified AI replay or live inference. Phase 1 acceptance gate: understand product and complete teaching flow within 60 seconds. Later phases require real Nemotron evidence interpretation and correction parsing through Nebius before hackathon submission.

Primary positioning: Best Apps and Agents. Official rules reviewed 2026-10-14: https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules . Runtime calls to Token Factory qualify; NVIDIA open-source model use is required. Nebius deployment is encouraged, not required. Submission also needs a public open-source repository, working demo URL and video under three minutes. None are claimed complete by Phase 1.


## Superseding architecture addendum

MarketBell is an always-on event-driven investing agent. It asks: **Has anything happened that I should care about, and what should I do about it?** Completed-session analysis is one scheduled input, not the main trigger.

Always watching → lightweight detection/deduplication/triage → selective Nemotron investigation → blind Challenge → deterministic authority → persisted outcome → rare interruption → explicitly approved learning.

Four channels: market-hours price/volume/news; outside-hours announcements/earnings/filings; scheduled portfolio health and opportunity reviews; user portfolio/preference/investigation triggers. Sources have configurable cadences and honest health/entitlement labels. No 24/7 coverage, real-time feed or uptime guarantee is made. The backend supports continuous operation independently of an open browser.

See docs/CONTINUOUS_OPERATION.md and the recorded browser-closed acceptance proof. The local implementation now supersedes the Phase 1-only scope described historically above.
